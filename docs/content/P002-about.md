# Professionalising the leadership of AI in Australian organisations.
ICAIO exists to develop the professional standards, capabilities, community, knowledge and practices required by executives responsible for AI strategy, transformation, governance, capability, adoption and value creation.

### 1. Who ICAIO is for
## The professional body for Australia's enterprise AI leaders.
ICAIO represents an executive accountability, not only a job title. It is being established for leaders who carry enterprise-wide responsibility for AI, including:
- Chief AI Officers
- Chief Data & AI Officers
- Heads of Enterprise AI
- CIOs, CTOs or CDOs with enterprise AI accountability
- Chief Transformation Officers or equivalent executives with enterprise-wide AI responsibility

ICAIO is not a vendor association, a consultancy or a technology community. It is being established as an independent, not-for-profit professional institution that reinvests any surplus in its purpose.

### 2. What ICAIO will do
## Four priorities for the profession.
The work is organised around the eight domains of Enterprise AI Leadership.
1. **Define the profession.** Develop shared accountabilities, competencies and professional standards for enterprise AI leadership.
2. **Build the executive community.** Bring together practitioners across sectors to exchange experience and help shape the discipline.
3. **Develop practical knowledge.** Build a Body of Knowledge, frameworks and research grounded in enterprise practice.
4. **Develop a representative voice.** Create a foundation for enterprise AI leaders to contribute informed perspectives to policy and professional debate.

### 3. Standards and education
## Setting the standard, working with educators.
ICAIO will keep setting professional standards separate from delivering education.
1. **Standards.** ICAIO defines the competencies, Body of Knowledge, learning outcomes and professional standards for enterprise AI leadership.
2. **Education.** Universities and other education providers may design programs aligned to those requirements.
3. **Recognition.** ICAIO may recognise appropriate external programs under provider-neutral rules.
4. **Designation.** Any future professional designation would assess experience, competence and continuing professional development, not course completion alone.

Callout: Certification will not be rushed. The professional standard, practitioner community, Body of Knowledge and evidence base come first.

### 5. Governance
## Independent governance from the outset.
ICAIO's intended governance model separates the institution's leadership, its Board and its practitioners.
1. **Independent Chair.** An independent Chair, separate from the Founder and executive leadership.
2. **Board.** A Board of approximately five to seven directors with formal governance duties.
3. **Founding Council.** A Founding Council of senior practitioners, distinct from the legal Board.
4. **Independence from sponsors.** Sponsors and vendors do not control professional standards or research.
5. **Related parties.** Any transaction with a Founder-related entity is disclosed, independently approved on arm's-length terms, with the Founder excluded from the decision.
6. **Ownership.** Professional standards and frameworks belong to ICAIO.

The independent Chair and Board will be announced as they are appointed.

### 6. How ICAIO is being established
## Built in stages, with credibility first.
The establishment sequence puts practitioners, frameworks and evidence in place before a public launch. ICAIO is currently at Stage 1.
