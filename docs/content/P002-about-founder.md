### Founder statement (Nicholas, first person)

For more than 25 years, my work has been about re-imagining business with emerging technology: using it to transform how organisations operate, to create new products and to build new business models that change markets.

AI is the most significant of these shifts so far. It changes strategy, operating models, risk and the workforce at once, and executives are being made accountable for it before the profession has agreed what good looks like.

I am establishing ICAIO so that the people carrying that accountability can define the standards, knowledge and community the role needs, and so that Australian organisations turn AI into market impact and lasting commercial returns.

Nicholas Dalla Riva, Founder and Chief Executive Officer

### About Nicholas (third person)

Nicholas Dalla Riva has spent more than 25 years leading digital and technology-led business transformation, new products and new business models, across financial services, technology and growth markets.

At NAB he led digital strategy and marketing, then direct sales and service strategy, before proposing and founding the digital capability for UBank, NAB's branchless bank. He secured $20 million in initial funding and developed a three-minute online loan application that simplified identity verification in Australian banking, laying the foundations for a digital bank that has since grown to more than one million customers. He later turned around the venture-backed comparison platform HelpMeChoose.com.au, taking it from a $2.4 million annual loss to break-even in nine months, and in 2009 founded Vocalise, one of Australia's first integrated growth marketing agencies.

In 2026 he completed Cornell University's Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer Program, alongside executive education in AI governance, risk and transformation from Cornell and the University of Oxford's Saïd Business School. He holds a BA in Philosophy and Politics from the University of Melbourne and a Postgraduate Diploma in eCommerce from Monash University.
