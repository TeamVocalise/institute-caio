# One discipline. Eight connected domains.
Enterprise AI leadership integrates these disciplines to convert rapidly evolving AI capability into sustainable organisational performance.

Domain grid band: Eight domains, one discipline. Each will be refined with the Founding Council. Link: Read each domain

## What enterprise AI leaders are accountable for.
A working description of each domain, offered as a starting point for the Founding Council to refine.

### 01 Ethical AI (For Boards)
The role of AI, AGI, ASI and QI in humanity and society, and how organisations determine and define their position.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Set the organisation's position on AI's role in society, and keep it current as capability advances towards AGI and beyond.
- Decide where AI should and should not be used, and the human oversight it requires.
- Give the board a clear view of the ethical questions AI raises for the organisation.

The question to answer: What is our position on AI, and would we be comfortable defending it publicly?

### 02 Governance
Responsible AI, risk, assurance, accountability, regulation and board oversight.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Establish AI risk, assurance and accountability frameworks.
- Align AI use with regulation and emerging standards.
- Give the board clear oversight of AI use, performance and incidents.

The question to answer: Who is accountable when an AI system gets it wrong?

### 03 Strategy
Enterprise AI strategy, portfolio choices, investment and alignment.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Set the enterprise AI strategy and investment portfolio.
- Align AI priorities with the organisation's strategy.
- Decide where to build, buy or partner.

The question to answer: Where will AI create the most advantage for us over the next three years?

### 04 Transformation
Operating-model and process transformation enabled by AI.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Redesign operating models and processes around AI.
- Sequence change across functions and business units.
- Manage the dependencies between technology, people and process.

The question to answer: Which parts of how we work should be redesigned, not just automated?

### 05 Value
Business cases, benefits, productivity, growth and value realisation.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Build credible business cases for AI investment.
- Track benefits, productivity and growth against those cases.
- Scale or stop initiatives on the evidence.

The question to answer: How do we know our AI investment is paying off?

### 06 Capability
Data, platforms, models, agents, architecture, vendors and organisational capability.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Ensure the data, platforms and architecture AI depends on.
- Make sound model, agent and vendor choices.
- Build the organisation's AI skills and teams.

The question to answer: Do we have the data, platforms and people to deliver our AI ambitions?

### 07 Adoption
Workforce, skills, change, behaviours and organisational adoption.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Lead workforce skills and change.
- Shape the behaviours and ways of working AI requires.
- Measure uptake and sustained, safe use.

The question to answer: Are our people using AI well, and safely?

### 08 Operating AI
The continual management, improvement and optimisation of the operation of AI models, agents and systems, workflows, and outputs.

Leaders are accountable for:
- Monitor the performance of models, agents and workflows in operation.
- Continually improve and optimise their outputs.
- Manage cost, reliability and change over time.

The question to answer: Are our AI systems still performing as intended, and getting better?

## A position before a plan. (Why Ethical AI comes first)
Ethical AI addresses the philosophical role of artificial intelligence as it relates to humanity and society, from today's systems through artificial general intelligence (AGI), artificial superintelligence (ASI) and quantum intelligence (QI).

It asks each organisation to determine and define its position on AI before planning how to use it. That position then guides every other domain.
