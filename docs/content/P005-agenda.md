# Start with the foundations of professional practice.
The proposed initial agenda focuses on five outputs. Their scope and priorities will be shaped with founding participants.

### 1. Initial outputs
## Five proposed starting points.
1. **Professional Charter.** A shared statement of the profession's purpose, principles and responsibilities.
2. **CAIO Competency Framework.** A framework for the capabilities required to lead enterprise AI.
3. **AI Leadership & Accountability Framework.** Clarity on leadership responsibilities, governance and accountability for enterprise AI.
4. **Enterprise AI Maturity Model.** A basis for assessing organisational progress and identifying priorities for development.
5. **State of Enterprise AI Leadership in Australia.** A proposed research report examining how the discipline is developing in Australian organisations.

### 2. The founding year
## Built in four stages with the Founding Council.
The initial outputs are proposed to be developed over the Council's 12-month founding term.
- Stage 1, **Define:** Founding Council, Professional Charter and the Enterprise AI Leadership definition.
- Stage 2, **Standardise:** Competency Framework 1.0 and the AI Leadership & Accountability Framework.
- Stage 3, **Benchmark:** An Australian enterprise AI leadership study and executive benchmarking.
- Stage 4, **Publish:** State of Enterprise AI Leadership in Australia, and an inaugural executive forum.

Link: About the Founding Council

### 3. The wider programme
## Potential first-wave professional knowledge.
Beyond the initial five, ICAIO has identified a wider body of work for the profession. All items are proposed. None has been published.

| Group | Description | Items (tags) |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | The shared definition and statement of the profession. | ICAIO Professional Charter (Initial); Enterprise AI Leadership definition; ICAIO Body of Knowledge |
| Roles and accountability | Who is accountable for what, and how decisions are shared across the executive team. | CAIO Role & Accountability Framework (Governance); AI Leadership & Accountability Framework (Initial, Governance); CAIO, CIO, CTO, CDO and CRO decision-rights model (Governance, Strategy) |
| Capability and maturity | What competence looks like for leaders, and maturity for organisations. | CAIO Competency Framework (Initial); Enterprise AI Maturity Model (Initial) |
| Domain frameworks | Practical frameworks for specific domains of Enterprise AI Leadership. | Ethical AI Position Framework (Ethical AI); AI Investment, Value & Performance Framework (Value); Agentic AI Governance Framework (Governance, Operating AI); AI Workforce & Adoption Framework (Adoption) |
| Evidence | Research into how the discipline is developing in Australian organisations. | State of Enterprise AI Leadership in Australia (Initial) |

### 4. How the work will be done
## Credible, independent and practitioner-led.
1. **Practitioner-led.** Developed with the executives who carry enterprise AI accountability, starting with the Founding Council.
2. **Evidence-based.** Grounded in enterprise practice and credible research, with methods and sources stated.
3. **Independent.** Sponsors and vendors do not control professional standards or research findings.
4. **Owned by the profession.** Professional standards and frameworks belong to ICAIO.
