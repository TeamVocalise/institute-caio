### 2. Why now

## Australia cannot afford to follow.

In the internet era, Australian organisations could watch, learn and adopt what worked elsewhere. In an era of abundant intelligence, no country has that luxury. Gartner predicts that by 2029, a quarter of Global 500 companies will continuously release AI-powered offerings, making fast-follower strategies obsolete.[1]

Much of the focus on AI is still on machine learning and data, but the role is changing. Gartner predicts that by 2027, 75% of chief data and analytics officers not seen as essential to their organisation's AI success will lose their C-level position,[2] and that by 2030, 80% of Global 500 companies will contractually make their CIO or Chief AI Officer the "Evidence Custodian" for AI accountability.[1] The remit, responsibility and accountability of AI leaders is broadening to whole-of-business transformation.

ICAIO is being established to help develop the shared standards, competencies and practices that this broader accountability requires.

### 3. Enterprise AI Leadership

Anchor: `enterprise-ai-leadership`

## One discipline. Eight connected domains.

Enterprise AI leadership integrates these disciplines to convert rapidly evolving AI capability into sustainable organisational performance.

### 4. Eight domains

#### Ethical AI

**For Boards:** The role of AI, AGI, ASI and QI in humanity and society, and how organisations determine and define their position.

#### Governance

Responsible AI, risk, assurance, accountability, regulation and board oversight.

#### Strategy

Enterprise AI strategy, portfolio choices, investment and alignment.

#### Transformation

Operating-model and process transformation enabled by AI.

#### Value

Business cases, benefits, productivity, growth and value realisation.

#### Capability

Data, platforms, models, agents, architecture, vendors and organisational capability.

#### Adoption

Workforce, skills, change, behaviours and organisational adoption.

#### Operating AI

The continual management, improvement and optimisation of the operation of AI models, agents and systems, workflows, and outputs.

### 5. Why ICAIO

## A professional institution for enterprise AI leadership.

ICAIO's purpose is to professionalise the leadership of artificial intelligence in Australian organisations. Its proposed work has four priorities.

#### Define the profession

Develop shared accountabilities, competencies and professional standards for enterprise AI leadership.

#### Build the executive community

Bring together practitioners across sectors to exchange experience and help shape the discipline.

#### Develop practical knowledge

Build a Body of Knowledge, frameworks and research grounded in enterprise practice.

#### Develop a representative voice

Create a foundation for enterprise AI leaders to contribute informed perspectives to policy and professional debate.

Text link: **About ICAIO** → `/about/`

### 6. Founding Council

## Help define an emerging profession.

ICAIO is bringing together a Founding Council of senior practitioners to help shape enterprise AI leadership in Australia.

The proposed Council will help define the profession's accountabilities, competencies, Body of Knowledge and research priorities. Its work will inform the institution as it develops.

We welcome expressions of interest from executives with enterprise AI accountability across Australian business, government and higher education. Membership is by invitation, for a 12-month founding term.

The Founding Council is distinct from the legal Board. An expression of interest does not guarantee an invitation, Council membership or a director appointment.

### 8. Independence

## Professional credibility starts with independence.

ICAIO is being developed around six founding principles. The intended model keeps professional standards and research independent of vendor and sponsor control.

#### Practitioner-led

Shaped by executives accountable for enterprise AI.

#### Independent

Vendors and sponsors do not control standards or research.

#### Evidence-based

Grounded in enterprise practice and credible research.

#### Responsible

Focused on value creation while keeping AI governable.

#### Collaborative

Complements rather than attempts to own the entire Australian AI ecosystem.

#### Profession-first

Institutional credibility takes priority over rapid monetisation.
