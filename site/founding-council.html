Founding Council

Help define the emerging profession of enterprise AI leadership in Australia.

ICAIO is assembling a Founding Council of senior practitioners to help establish the profession, rather than endorse an organisation that is already fully designed.

The role

What the Council may help define.

Page shell. Structure and draft content drawn from the project context for review. Full page copy has not yet been written or approved.

  • Enterprise AI Leadership and the CAIO role and accountability framework
  • A professional competency framework and Body of Knowledge
  • Education and professional development requirements
  • Designation requirements, if later adopted
  • The research agenda and an enterprise AI maturity model
  • Governance, accountability, value and performance frameworks

What to expect

A clear, proportionate commitment.

  • Size and mix. Approximately 12 to 20 senior practitioners drawn deliberately across sectors, including financial services, government, healthcare, telecommunications, retail, resources, professional services, technology and higher education.
  • Time commitment. [Format, frequency and expected hours to be confirmed]
  • Personal capacity. Members take part in a personal capacity and do not represent their employers unless explicitly approved.
  • Not a Board seat. Council membership does not make a person a legal director.
  • Remuneration. [Position to be confirmed]
  • Selection. [Selection approach and criteria to be confirmed]
  • Next steps. [What happens after an expression of interest, and response timing]
