Proposed agenda

Start with the foundations of professional practice.

The proposed initial agenda focuses on five outputs. Their scope and priorities will be shaped with founding participants.

Initial outputs

Five proposed starting points.

  1. Professional Charter

    A shared statement of the profession's purpose, principles and responsibilities.

  2. CAIO Competency Framework

    A framework for the capabilities required to lead enterprise AI.

  3. Leadership & Accountability Framework

    Clarity on leadership responsibilities, governance and accountability for enterprise AI.

  4. Enterprise AI Maturity Model

    A basis for assessing organisational progress and identifying priorities for development.

  5. State of Enterprise AI Leadership in Australia

    A proposed research report examining how the discipline is developing in Australian organisations.
