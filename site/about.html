About ICAIO

Professionalising the leadership of AI in Australian organisations.

ICAIO exists to develop the professional standards, capabilities, community, knowledge and practices required by executives responsible for AI strategy, transformation, governance, capability, adoption and value creation.

Positioning

The professional body for Australia's enterprise AI leaders.

Page shell. Structure and draft content drawn from the project context for review. Full page copy has not yet been written or approved.

ICAIO represents an executive accountability, not only the job title Chief AI Officer. Leaders it is intended for include:

  • Chief AI Officers
  • Chief Data & AI Officers
  • Heads of Enterprise AI
  • CIOs, CTOs or CDOs with enterprise AI accountability
  • Chief Transformation Officers or equivalent executives with enterprise-wide AI responsibility

Governance commitments

  • An independent Chair, separate from the Founder and executive leadership.
  • A Board of approximately five to seven directors with formal governance duties.
  • A Founding Council of senior practitioners, distinct from the legal Board.
  • Sponsors and vendors do not control professional standards or research.
  • Any transaction with a Founder-related entity is disclosed, independently approved on arm's-length terms, with the Founder excluded from the decision.
  • Professional standards and frameworks belong to ICAIO.

How ICAIO is being established

Built in stages, with credibility first.

The establishment sequence puts practitioners, frameworks and evidence in place before a public launch.

Stage 1

Coalition

Recruit a small number of credible leaders and establish the Founding Council.

Stage 2

Define

Define the profession, charter, accountabilities and initial competency framework.

Stage 3

Evidence

Conduct research and benchmarking; develop substantive professional knowledge.

Stage 4

Foundation membership

Invite an initial, carefully selected cohort of enterprise AI leaders.

Stage 5

Public launch

Launch with credible leaders, research and professional frameworks in place.

Founding principles

Professional credibility starts with independence.

Practitioner-led

Shaped by executives accountable for enterprise AI.

Independent

Vendors and sponsors do not control standards or research.

Evidence-based

Grounded in enterprise practice and credible research.

Responsible

Focused on value creation while keeping AI governable.

Collaborative

Complements rather than attempts to own the entire Australian AI ecosystem.

Profession-first

Institutional credibility takes priority over rapid monetisation.

ICAIO

The Institute of Chief AI Officers is being established as an independent, not-for-profit professional institution for executives accountable for enterprise AI.
