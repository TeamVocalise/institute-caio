# ICAIO Project Context

> Updated 12 October 2026: name changed to Institute of Chief AI Officers (ICAIO), and the domain model expanded from six to eight. Website decisions are logged in docs/04-approved-decisions.md.

## Project
**Name:** Institute of Chief AI Officers (ICAIO). Decided 12 October 2026, replacing the working name Association of Chief AI Officers Australia (ACAIO).
**Domain:** caio.org.au (registered).

ICAIO is being developed as an independent, not-for-profit professional institution for executives accountable for enterprise artificial intelligence in Australian organisations.

This file is persistent project context for Codex. Treat items marked **DECIDED** as constraints unless explicitly changed by the project owner. Items marked **OPEN** are unresolved and must not be silently invented.

## Purpose
**To professionalise the leadership of artificial intelligence in Australian organisations.**

ICAIO exists to develop the professional standards, capabilities, community, knowledge and practices required by executives responsible for AI strategy, transformation, governance, capability, adoption and value creation.

## Positioning
Working positioning:
**The professional body for Australia's enterprise AI leaders.**

ICAIO represents an executive accountability, not merely the literal job title "Chief AI Officer".

Eligible leaders may include:
- Chief AI Officer
- Chief Data & AI Officer
- Head of Enterprise AI
- CIO, CTO or CDO with enterprise AI accountability
- Chief Transformation Officer or equivalent executive with enterprise-wide AI responsibility

Do not position ICAIO as:
- a general AI enthusiast community
- a developer community
- an AI vendor association
- a consultancy
- an AI hype/news website
- primarily a certification seller

## Enterprise AI Leadership Model
The emerging professional discipline is organised around eight domains (updated 12 October 2026):

1. **Ethical AI** — For Boards: the role of AI, AGI, ASI and QI in humanity and society, and how organisations determine and define their position.
2. **Governance** — responsible AI, risk, assurance, accountability, regulation and board oversight.
3. **Strategy** — enterprise AI strategy, portfolio choices, investment and alignment.
4. **Transformation** — operating-model and process transformation enabled by AI.
5. **Value** — business cases, benefits, productivity, growth and value realisation.
6. **Capability** — data, platforms, models, agents, architecture, vendors and organisational capability.
7. **Adoption** — workforce, skills, change, behaviours and organisational adoption.
8. **Operating AI** — the continual management, improvement and optimisation of the operation of AI models, agents and systems, workflows, and outputs.

The Ethical AI and Operating AI definitions are Nick's. Do not refer to the Gorilla problem in public copy (Nick, 12 October 2026).

Core proposition:
**Enterprise AI leadership integrates these disciplines to convert rapidly evolving AI capability into sustainable organisational performance.**

## Institutional Model
**DECIDED:** ICAIO is intended to be an independent not-for-profit professional institution rather than a founder-owned commercial association.

Likely legal form to investigate with Australian legal/tax advisers: company limited by guarantee.

The institution should be commercially sustainable while reinvesting surpluses into its purpose.

Potential revenue streams:
- executive membership
- corporate membership
- strategic partnerships/sponsorship
- executive education and professional development
- events and executive forums
- research and benchmarking
- publications/data products
- accreditation/certification if justified later

Not-for-profit does not mean volunteer-run. ICAIO should be capable of employing professional management and paying market-based remuneration as finances permit.

## Founding Council
The Founding Council is intended to help establish the profession rather than simply endorse an organisation already fully designed.

Its role may include helping define:
- Enterprise AI Leadership
- CAIO role/accountability framework
- professional competency framework
- Body of Knowledge
- education requirements
- professional-development requirements
- certification/designation requirements, if later adopted
- research agenda
- enterprise AI maturity model
- governance/accountability framework
- AI value and performance framework

Council members should be drawn from a deliberately diverse cross-section such as financial services, government, healthcare, telecommunications, retail, resources, professional services, technology and higher education.

The invitation should be framed as:
**Help define the emerging profession of enterprise AI leadership in Australia.**

## Education, Accreditation and Universities
ICAIO should distinguish between **setting professional standards** and **delivering education**.

Preferred model:
1. ICAIO defines competencies, Body of Knowledge, learning outcomes and professional standards.
2. Universities and other education providers may design programs aligned to those requirements.
3. ICAIO may recognise/accredit appropriate external programs under provider-neutral rules.
4. Professional designation/certification, if created, should assess more than course completion and may include experience, competence and continuing professional development.
5. ICAIO may itself offer professional development where governance permits.

**DECIDED:** Do not launch certification prematurely.

Build the professional standard, practitioner community, Body of Knowledge and evidence base first.

## Related Commercial Activity
The Founder may separately create commercial education or intellectual property outside ICAIO.

Any transaction between ICAIO and a Founder-owned entity must be treated as a related-party transaction with strong governance:
- full disclosure
- arm's-length terms
- independent board approval
- founder recusal from the decision
- appropriate market testing/benchmarking
- clear IP ownership
- documented benefit to ICAIO

**DECIDED (12 October 2026):** The ICAIO website project will move into the new ICAIO entity and form part of the CEO role. It is not a Vocalise engagement.

Core ICAIO institutional IP—such as ICAIO professional standards, competency frameworks and accreditation requirements—should belong to ICAIO.

A separately developed commercial education program may potentially be licensed to ICAIO or other customers, subject to proper governance.

## Founding Principles
- **Practitioner-led** — shaped by executives accountable for enterprise AI.
- **Independent** — vendors and sponsors do not control standards or research.
- **Evidence-based** — grounded in enterprise practice and credible research.
- **Responsible** — focused on value creation while keeping AI governable.
- **Collaborative** — complements rather than attempts to own the entire Australian AI ecosystem.
- **Profession-first** — institutional credibility takes priority over rapid monetisation.

## Establishment Strategy
Preferred sequence:

### Stage 1 — Coalition
Recruit a small number of credible leaders and establish the Founding Council.

### Stage 2 — Define
Work with the Council to define the profession, charter, accountabilities and initial competency framework.

### Stage 3 — Evidence
Conduct research and benchmarking; develop substantive intellectual property.

### Stage 4 — Foundation Membership
Invite an initial carefully selected cohort of enterprise AI leaders.

### Stage 5 — Public Launch
Launch the institution publicly with credible leaders, research and professional frameworks already in place.

The desired launch posture is not:
"We started an association. Would you like to join?"

It is closer to:
"Australian enterprise AI leaders have worked together to define an emerging professional discipline. Here is the resulting framework and institution."
